Secure Group

As allianceauth-secure-groups: Tether keeps a smart group's members by its filters. Every filter must pass (a reversed one must fail). Auto groups add everyone who passes; others take requests, from the Groups or Secure Groups page, only from those who pass. Members who stop passing leave at once, or when a failing filter's grace period ends if the group can grace.
