## Alliance Auth and its community apps

Tether reimplements what Alliance Auth and several of its community apps do, from their documentation and behaviour, so that alliances moving off Alliance Auth find the same rules, permissions and names. It contains no code from them. A few short texts are the same as theirs on purpose:

- permission descriptions, so admins recognise the permissions they grant;
- notification type names;
- a handful of user-facing sentences.

`docs/LEGAL.md` lists how this was checked.

Where such text comes from an MIT-licensed app, that app's notice follows, as its license asks.
